      How the next best action is chosen

      Rules first, then a score. Hard rules decide what is allowed; a simple score picks among what’s left; every response updates the inputs for the next decision. All weights below are illustrative starting points that get tuned with holdout tests.
      1 · GateCredit-eligible + ITA, risk, consent, quiet hours, weekly cap, snooze
      2 · StreamThe strongest signal sets the stream (P1)
      3 · CandidatesChannels allowed in that stream, at their next good time
      4 · ScoreAffinity × timing × stage value × concern match − costs
      5 · Pick or waitHighest score wins; below 0.03 the engine waits
      6 · LearnResponse or silence updates intent, affinity and concern
      The score
      score = affinity(channel) × timing × stage value × concern match − fatigue − opt-out risk affinity learned response rate for the channel start: in-product 0.55 · email 0.45 · push 0.30 · SMS 0.25 ignored touch × 0.85 · click or tap × 1.2 (max 0.9) timing 1.0 at their moment (email 7–8 AM, in session, minutes after leaving) · 0.8 otherwise stage value ITA only 0.3 · Credit signal 0.5 Customer signal 0.7 · Apply and recover 1.0 concern 1.2 when the message answers the likely concern fatigue 0.02 × touches in the last 7 days opt-out base(channel) × (1 + misses in a row) × 5 base: email 0.002 · push 0.002 · SMS 0.004 · in-product 0 send if score ≥ 0.03, else wait
